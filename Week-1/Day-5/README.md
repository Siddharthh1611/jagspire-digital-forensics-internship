# Day 5 – Digital Forensics Workflow & Case Study

## 📌 Objective
Understand the real-world digital forensics investigation process and the role of AI in forensic analysis.

---

## 🔍 Digital Forensics Workflow

Digital forensics follows a structured process to ensure proper investigation and legal validity.

### 1. Evidence Identification
- Identify potential sources of digital evidence  
- Examples: computers, mobile devices, logs, cloud storage  

---

### 2. Evidence Collection
- Collect data without altering original evidence  
- Tools used:
  - FTK Imager  
  - EnCase  
  - dd (Linux)  

---

### 3. Evidence Preservation
- Maintain integrity of evidence  
- Use hashing (MD5/SHA)  
- Maintain chain of custody  

---

### 4. Evidence Analysis
- Analyze collected data to find relevant information  
- Techniques:
  - Log analysis  
  - Memory forensics  
  - File recovery  
  - Malware analysis  

---

### 5. Reporting
- Document findings clearly  
- Include:
  - Tools used  
  - Steps followed  
  - Evidence found  
  - Conclusion  

---

## 🤖 Role of AI in Digital Forensics

Artificial Intelligence enhances forensic investigations by:

- Automating log analysis  
- Detecting anomalies and patterns  
- Identifying malware behavior  
- Speeding up evidence processing  
- Assisting in large-scale data analysis  

---

## 📖 Case Study (Example)

### 🔐 Scenario:
A system shows unusual login activity.

### 🔍 Investigation Steps:
1. Collected system logs  
2. Analyzed login timestamps  
3. Detected multiple failed login attempts  
4. Found unauthorized access from unknown IP  

### 📌 Conclusion:
- System was targeted by brute-force attack  
- Unauthorized access was successfully detected  

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- Digital forensics follows a structured workflow  
- Evidence integrity is critical in investigations  
- AI improves speed and accuracy of analysis  
- Proper reporting is essential for legal use
