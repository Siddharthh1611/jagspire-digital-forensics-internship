## 🧠 One-Page Summary: What is Digital Forensics?

Digital Forensics is the process of identifying, collecting, preserving, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence in a legally acceptable manner. It is widely used in cybercrime investigations, corporate security, and incident response.

The process begins with identifying potential sources of evidence such as computers, mobile devices, or network logs. The data is then carefully collected without altering its integrity. Preservation techniques like hashing and maintaining chain of custody ensure that the evidence remains untampered.

During analysis, investigators examine the data to uncover hidden information, detect malicious activities, and reconstruct events. Techniques such as log analysis, memory forensics, and malware analysis are commonly used.

Finally, the findings are documented in a structured report, which can be used in legal proceedings or organizational decision-making.
